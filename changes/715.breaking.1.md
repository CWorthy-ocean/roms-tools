ESPER-derived tracers are now groomed by default, so ESPER initial conditions and boundary forcing change wherever cells are flagged; pass `groom=False` to `InitialConditions`/`BoundaryForcing` for the raw estimates.
* A YAML written by roms-tools 5.0–5.3 has no `groom` key and loads with grooming on; add `groom: false` to its section to skip grooming.

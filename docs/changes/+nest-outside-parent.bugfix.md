Regenerate any child grid that extends beyond its parent grid over land and that you aligned with roms-tools 3.3.0 through 5.4.0, together with the forcing you made on it: the grid's depth `h` was NaN at the land points outside the parent, and ucla-roms stops with a BLOWUP at the first step on such a grid. Those points now keep the child grid's own depth and mask, and `align_grids` logs a warning with their count.
* `align_grids` now raises an error if any ocean point of the child grid lies outside the parent grid, including points behind a closed boundary. Before, only ocean points on open boundaries were checked.

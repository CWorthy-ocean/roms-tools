**Every `SurfaceForcing` dataset, and so every saved surface-forcing file, now carries its own recipe in a `roms_tools_yaml` global attribute.** It is the YAML that `to_yaml` writes, including any local source paths. This is what lets a blend of saved files record how they were made. Writing that YAML goes through a new `yaml_text` helper, and `write_to_yaml` now uses it too.
